Outbound Dial Cheat Sheet

Black — say this Blue — backing context, don't read aloud

Outbound dial to a parent who's applied, or who's been through part of the sales process before. Short call, 5–10 minutes. The only goal is to book the next call, which in almost every case is a PDS. For the full discovery, use the PDS Script.

5. Frame, qualify and book

Order: frame → price → decision maker → booking → resources. Set the frame first so the Academy feels like something they want to get access to, then qualify on price.

Frame

No PDS yet.

  • So the next step from here is a call with one of our team, where we go into more depth on [player]'s game and whether [she/he] would be a fit for the Academy.
  • As with any academy, there's a lot of support that goes in from our side, so we're quite careful who we offer places to. This call is really to make sure it's a fit at a high level.
  • All clear on that?

Already had a PDS or PEM. This is the PDS running as a PEM, so the player needs to be on it and it can be a one-call close.

  • So the next step from here is a call with one of our team, where we pick up from where you left off with [rep name] and go back into [player]'s game.
  • Given you've already been through a lot of the background, we can spend most of that call on [player] directly. And if it's a fit, we can get [her/him] started on the call, so you're not waiting around.
  • It's best [player] is on that call too, as [she/he]'ll actually get to experience some of the coaching live.
  • As with any academy, we're quite careful who we offer places to, so the call is still there to make sure it's the right fit.
  • All clear on that?

Price

Qualification vs DQ — the rule. A family qualifies financially if they can access $3,500 USD to start: either as liquid funds they have now, or through credit they are both willing and able to use. If they can access neither, that's a financial DQ. Credit counts — the test is access to the money, not where it comes from.

We don't know their financial situation at this point, so run this with everyone and read how much friction comes back. Go deeper where needed.

  • And have you invested in private coaching or any paid development programmes beyond club fees for [player] before?

Decision maker

  • Is there anyone else who'd be involved in decisions about [player]'s development, like another parent or guardian?

If yes: "Ok great, it's best they're on the call too. Do you know their availability?" If they don't: book a time that works for you two, and ask them to confirm with the partner in the meantime.

This matters even more here. If the partner wasn't on the last call, that's often why it didn't close.

Booking

Book the soonest slot that still leaves time for the resources. Offer only 2–3 options. Book in an INCOGNITO WINDOW using THEIR TIMEZONE, or the CRM pulls cached data from other prospects and their reminder texts show the wrong time. Never say "I'll email you the links". Lock the date and time before hanging up.

  • So I'm pulling up the calendar here. What timezone are you in?
  • Ok, so I have [time] or [time] on [day], or [time] on [day]. Do any of those work?
  • Great, and I have [email] as your email address. Is that the best one for you?
  • Ok, that's booked for you. Could you check your email to make sure you received it, and that the event is in your calendar? Different inboxes sometimes don't add it automatically.
  • Oh nice, so you're actually booked with [name]. [She/He] works really closely with Coach Garrett and our players and families.

Flag it to the rep before the call so they know to run the PDS as a PEM and go for the close.

Hype the resources

  • Before we jump off, it's really important to spend 10–15 minutes going through the resources on how we train players before your call. In particular [XYZ resource], which gives you more context on how this works, so [name] can spend the call applying it to [player] instead of explaining the basics.
  • Will you be able to go through that before the call? Great, I'll send you the link now.

6. If it's not a fit

Disqualify respectfully and clearly. No ambiguity.

Player fit DQ

  • Ok, so to be very transparent, based on what you've mentioned about [player]'s situation right now, this isn't something we'd be able to help with.
  • Coach Garrett has a lot of free content on our website that helps players improve their decision-making on the field, which could really help [her/him] in the meantime.
  • Thank you for your time, and all the best.

Financial DQ

  • Ok, so to be very transparent, based on what you've mentioned about resources, this won't be something we can help with right now.
  • It sounds like [XYZ] is something we can help [player] with, but it would need to be at a point where you're able to make the investment before we look at booking a call with the team.
  • In the meantime, Coach Garrett has a lot of free content on our website that helps players improve their decision-making on the field, which could really help [her/him] get closer to where you want [her/him] to be.
  • Thank you for your time, and all the best.

7. Close out

  • That's everything from my side. Is there anything else I can help with while we're on the call?
  • Great, I'll send over the links, and all the best for your call with [rep name].
